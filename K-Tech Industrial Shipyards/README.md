# K-Tech Industrial shipyards 

- After speaking with several mining crews that often use the Rusatom-Antonoff-K255 Titan, the CEO of K-Tech Industrial decided to open a new shipyard department. K-Tech Industrial Shipyards has been tasked with overseeing the modification of existing ships to expand thier capabilities. 

# Mod notes: 
- The `K-Tech Industrial shipyards` department adds new ship variants to the game as separate mod files. 


## K225-KTI [right click > save as](https://github.com/Kadaire/K-Tech-Industrial-of-Mars/blob/Main/K-Tech%20Industrial%20Shipyards/K-T%20Industrial%20K225-KTI.zip)
- Adds a new AT-K225 variant: `K225-KTI`
- Approximate Cost: `5,000,000 E$`
- Secondary Aux Power Slot
- High stress Hardpoints: `2`
- Drone Hardpoints: `2`
- Low stress Hardpoints: `2`
- Processed Material Storage: `26,000Kg`
- Docking Bays: `6`

## K44 & K37 Series [right click > save as](https://github.com/Kadaire/K-Tech-Industrial-of-Mars/blob/Main/K-Tech%20Industrial%20Shipyards/K-T%20Industrial%20K-Series.zip)
- Adds Two new upgraded variants of the K37 & K44 Series ships.
- Approximate cost: Same as a standard K series.
- High Stress Hardpoints: `1`
- Low Stress Hardpoints: `2`
- Processed Material storage: K37: `7,000kg` K44: `10,000kg` 
- Docking bays: `2` Supports THI Cargo containers, and B8 Claim Beacons. Other modules not "officially" supported or covered by warranty.

## Kitsumo [right click > save as](https://github.com/Kadaire/K-Tech-Industrial-of-Mars/blob/Main/K-Tech%20Industrial%20Shipyards/K-T%20Industrial%20Kitsumo.zip)
- Adds a new AT-K225 variant: `Kitsumo-KTI`
- Approximate Cost: `600,000 E$`
- High stress Hardpoints: `1`
- Low stress Hardpoints: `1`
- Processed Material Storage: `36,000Kg`
- Main torch: `1`
- RCS: `6`

## OCP-230 KTI [right click > save as](https://github.com/Kadaire/K-Tech-Industrial-of-Mars/blob/Main/K-Tech%20Industrial%20Shipyards/K-T%20Industrial%20OCP230-KTI.zip)
- Adds a new OCP Variant
- Approximate cost `1.2M E$`
- Arm mounts remain unchanged, the primary weapon slots have been moved to the side, you can now fly like a crab AND fire both weapons at incoming roids.

## Cothon Mk2 KTI [right click > save as](https://github.com/Kadaire/K-Tech-Industrial-of-Mars/blob/Main/K-Tech%20Industrial%20Shipyards/K-T%20Industrial%20Cothon%20Mk2.zip)
- Adds TWO variants of the triple torch Cothon variant. (with slightly different RCS layouts between the two)
- Approximate cost `1M E$`
- internal capacity increased, RCS mounts slightly modified. 

# Important Compatiblity Note:
**`K-Tech Industrial` Branded Mods DO NOT currently work with the Industries of Enceladus mod. There is no current workaround for this. Will update this section if that changes.**
